## Course art direction

| Reference | What the island can borrow |
| --- | --- |
| Mario Kart Stadium and Sunshine Airport | A wide grid, overhead structure and repeating lines establish scale. Keep the start understandable with several karts abreast. |
| Toad Harbor and Coconut Mall | Boats, facades and grouped palm planters explain the place around the road. Group related objects at destinations and preserve sight lines through the apex. |
| Dolphin Shoals and Mount Wario | A coastal opening or change of elevation can reveal the next landscape. Give each major change of grade a view that rewards the climb. |
| Asphalt’s Caribbean and San Francisco | A warm beach and cool water make a readable coastal silhouette. A crest reads better when there is another layer of terrain beyond it. |
| Asphalt’s Singapore and Rome | Use reflected light selectively. Give a bridge or tunnel a recognizable outline that a driver can spot before reaching it. |
| Asphalt’s Himalayas | Separate foreground rock, middle-distance headlands and distant peaks through overlapping silhouettes and atmospheric color. |

The island’s harbor village, rock gallery and cable-stayed crossing give players three places to recognize at speed. Repeating architectural details link each group of props, while distant terrain gets softer colors. The course, karts, buildings and branding are authored in KartVerse. ## Implementation sequence

1. Establish the palette, type roles and control sizes. Apply the same treatment to the landing page, lobby, garage, results and HUD.
2. Author the island silhouette, elevation and landmarks in track coordinates. Align the start and finish gate with the grid, preserve checkpoint ordering and place boost pads on a drivable approach. Epic’s circuit validation illustrates the geometric contract between a primary route and the gates that measure it. [Track devices](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-rocket-racing-track-devices-in-unreal-editor-for-fortnite) · [Checkpoint devices](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-rocket-racing-checkpoint-devices-in-unreal-editor-for-fortnite)
3. Use Three.js arc-length sampling and shared geometry for repeated props. Keep the custom kart physics. R3F and Drei document the same draw-call savings through instancing. Rapier’s collider system provides a comparison for shape clearance, while KartVerse already owns road contact in its track and physics modules. [Three.js Curve](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Curve.html) · [Three.js InstancedMesh](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/InstancedMesh.html) · [R3F scaling](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/advanced/scaling-performance) · [Drei Instances](https://drei.docs.pmnd.rs/performances/instances) · [Rapier colliders](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/javascript/colliders/)
4. Reserve the center of the race view for upcoming road. Group held controls by thumb, use pointer capture and clear cancelled input. Leave room around the iPhone safe area and aim for 44-point primary controls. Respect reduced-motion preferences for interface transitions. [MDN Pointer Events](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Pointer_events) · [Apple game controls](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/game-controls) · [GSAP media queries](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/gsap.matchMedia()/)
5. Check the racing line and the two road edges over a whole lap, then cross the finish backwards, skip a gate and race with multiple clients. Compare the rendered course to the minimap at the grid, bridge, tunnel and hairpin.

## Asset provenance

The reference study imported no meshes, textures, screenshots, sound or source code into KartVerse. Old San Juan, Rally, Rush and pmndrs identify MIT code licenses. React-Kart had no root license file in the revision inspected. Screenshot and commercial course references were used only for study. The project's shipped asset inventory lives in [ASSET_LICENSES.md](../ASSET_LICENSES.md).

Kenney’s Racing Kit and Quaternius’s Stylized Nature MegaKit list CC0 on their pack pages. Poly Haven and ambientCG identify their downloadable assets as CC0. Sketchfab has model-specific terms, so a model needs its own license and attribution review. Three.js examples were consulted for rendering approaches. The procedural course keeps materials, geometry and scale under the same art direction.
